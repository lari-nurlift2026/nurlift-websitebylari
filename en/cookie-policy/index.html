Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026.

1. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files a website may store in your browser. Similar technologies can store information or support website features. Their purpose and lifetime depend on how they are implemented. This policy distinguishes website code from possible infrastructure operations.

2. What This Website Currently Uses

Nurlift does not currently implement analytics or advertising cookies in the website frontend. The current code does not explicitly write cookies or use localStorage, sessionStorage or IndexedDB for tracking. There is currently no Nurlift-operated cookie preference center or consent banner.

3. Essential Infrastructure Technologies

Essential hosting, security, CDN or infrastructure technologies may operate where necessary for security and functionality. The repository does not confirm the names, providers or lifetimes of infrastructure-level cookies. This neither confirms that such cookies are present nor means that the server keeps no technical logs.

4. Analytics and Advertising

Nurlift does not currently implement analytics, advertising pixels or marketing scripts in this website’s frontend. If non-essential analytics, advertising or other technologies are added later, this policy and any applicable preference controls will be updated before or alongside their implementation.

5. Current Cookie Inventory

The current status is described by category, without assigning names or expiry periods to unconfirmed cookies.

  • Analytics cookies: not currently implemented by Nurlift in the website frontend.
  • Advertising/marketing cookies: not currently implemented by Nurlift in the website frontend.
  • Essential infrastructure technologies: may exist depending on hosting and security infrastructure; specific names, providers and lifetimes are not confirmed in the repository.

6. Third-Party Services

The current code contains no embedded widgets or external analytics and marketing scripts. This does not rule out suppliers supporting hosting, security or technical operations. Email links may open services chosen by visitors, which operate under their own terms and policies.

7. Browser Controls

You may use your browser settings to review, block or delete cookies, depending on the controls available. Blocking essential technologies may affect functionality. These controls belong to your browser; the website does not currently offer its own preference center.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the technologies used. The current version and update date will be available on this page. Any introduction of non-essential technologies will be accompanied by updated information and applicable controls.

9. Contact and Privacy Policy

For questions, email contato@nurlift.com. Read our Privacy Policy for information about personal data processing and your rights.
