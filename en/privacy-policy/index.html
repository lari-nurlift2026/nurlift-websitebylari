Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026.

1. About This Privacy Policy

This policy explains how personal information is handled in connection with Nurlift’s corporate website and enquiries initiated by visitors. It does not replace specific privacy information provided for contracted products or services.

2. Who Controls Your Information

The controller is Nurlift Tecnologia Para Publicidade Ltda., registered in Brazil under CNPJ 32.211.857/0001-47, at Rua Joaquim Candido de Azevedo Marques, 1.121, Vila Morumbi, São Paulo, Brazil. Our contact and privacy email is contato@nurlift.com.

3. Personal Information We Collect

When you email us, we process the information you provide. Contact forms collect only name, email, telephone and subject. Please avoid including sensitive information or unnecessary information about other people. Hosting and server infrastructure may process technical information needed to operate and secure the website.

4. How We Collect It

You provide information directly when you contact us. Current email links open your email client; you decide whether to send a message. Information submitted through forms is validated by Nurlift’s controlled backend and forwarded to its email-based contact workflow.

5. Why We Use Your Information

We use information to respond to enquiries and contact requests, conduct commercial discussions and relevant follow-up, and keep the history needed for those interactions. Sending an enquiry does not automatically authorize newsletters or marketing campaigns unrelated to your request.

6. Legal Grounds

Depending on the purpose and circumstances, processing may rely on steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, legitimate interests where applicable and subject to your rights, or compliance with legal or regulatory obligations. Consent is not the default legal ground for every enquiry. Where needed for a specific purpose, it will be requested appropriately.

7. Service Providers and Sharing

Suppliers supporting hosting, infrastructure, security, email and technical operations may process information only to the extent needed to provide and secure the website and respond to requests. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or a competent authority. If a supplier’s international operations involve a transfer of personal information, applicable legal safeguards will be observed where required.

8. Retention

The contact backend forwards requests by email without creating a separate lead database. Information submitted through forms is retained within Nurlift’s email and contact workflow. Temporary technical records used for security and duplicate prevention do not store form field contents. Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, maintain the relevant commercial relationship, and meet legal, regulatory or evidentiary obligations or establish and exercise legal rights. Deletion depends on the continuing need for processing and applicable retention requirements; there is no single fixed period for every situation.

9. Security

Nurlift takes reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the information processed. No system is completely secure, and these measures do not guarantee that incidents can never occur.

10. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary, excessive or unlawfully processed information; information about sharing; portability where applicable; and objection where permitted by law. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it, ask about the option not to consent and its consequences, and request deletion subject to lawful retention exceptions. Requests are assessed under the conditions and time limits established by applicable law.

11. Children

If an enquiry involves a child’s or young person’s information, processing must respect their best interests and the specific requirements of applicable law. Parents or guardians may contact our privacy channel about information submitted by minors.

12. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The current website does not use advertising or analytics cookies implemented by Nurlift. Essential technical features of the hosting environment may operate as needed for security and functionality. This does not mean that the server keeps no technical logs.

13. External Links and Services

Email links may open external applications or services you choose to use. Those services handle information under their own terms and privacy policies. This policy describes processing for which Nurlift is responsible.

14. Policy Updates

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or the law. The updated version and its date will be available on this page, with additional notices where legally required.

15. Contact Us

For questions or requests concerning your personal information, email contato@nurlift.com. Describe your request and provide the information needed to locate the relevant interaction. We may ask for proportionate information to verify your identity and prevent unauthorized access. You may also contact Brazil’s ANPD or other competent authorities as provided by applicable law.
