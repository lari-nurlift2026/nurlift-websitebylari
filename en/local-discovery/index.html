Where Discovery Happens

The path to a decision spans more than one platform.

A customer might find a location on a map, check its opening hours on a business profile and look for more information elsewhere before calling or visiting.

Local Presence

Google Business Profile, Google Maps and Apple help people find businesses and look up information about individual locations.

Broader Discovery Signals

Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn can also shape how people see a brand through posts, conversations and references. They complement the role of maps and business profiles.

Each platform plays a different role. Your brand should remain recognizable across them.

Information Customers Act On

Accurate location data makes the next step easier.

Addresses, phone numbers, opening hours, categories and business details help customers understand where to find you, when to visit and what to expect.

Business listings and profiles should describe each location consistently across platforms. Conflicting details can make it harder to get in touch, lead to wasted trips and undermine trust.

Consistency starts with the details customers rely on.

Local Trust

Customer experiences help others decide.

Reviews and feedback help people form expectations about a location and decide whether it meets their needs.

Local reputation reflects everyday experiences: service, quality, convenience and whether a business delivers on its promise. Paying attention to this feedback helps identify strengths and areas that need attention at each location.

Presence helps customers find you. Trust helps them choose you.

One Brand, Multiple Locations

Brand consistency depends on every location.

As your network grows, keeping information accurate becomes more complex. Each location also has its own operating needs.

A consistent presence across locations depends on shared standards, clear ownership and attention to changes in addresses, phone numbers, opening hours and services. Governance connects brand standards with the day-to-day reality of each location.

Customers visit one location. Their experience shapes how they see the whole brand.
